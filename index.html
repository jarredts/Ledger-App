User Guide

Two calendars, one app

Toggle between Cash Calendar and Debt Calendar at the top. Cash shows your checking/savings balance day by day; Debt shows what each card or loan owes over time. Use the account filter dropdown to combine or isolate specific accounts within whichever calendar you're on.

Editing a recurring bill or paycheck

Tap a day, then the pencil next to any recurring item. Choose this occurrence only for a one-off change (skips just that date), or all future events to permanently change the amount going forward — this splits the schedule so past history stays untouched.

Debt payments & payoffs

Tapping the pencil on a debt payment opens a small override box for just that one payment — perfect for an extra or full payoff amount on a specific date. There's a link inside that box if you actually meant to change the debt's permanent settings (APR, minimum payment, end date) instead. Once a debt's balance actually reaches $0, future payments stop showing automatically — no manual end date needed.

Marking things paid

Every transaction — recurring or one-off, in the day view or the Log tab — has a checkbox to mark it Paid/Cleared. This is just for your own tracking; it doesn't change the forecast math, so it's safe to check things off early or late without throwing off future balances.

For bills or income you know happen automatically (direct deposit, autopay), check Autopay when adding or editing that recurring rule — it'll mark each occurrence Paid on its own once the date passes, so you're not manually checking off the same thing every cycle.

Keeping a balance accurate

Tap the wallet icon (top right) to manage accounts. Tapping an account's name lets you rename it or fix its original base balance. The icon next to it is different — use that anytime to reconcile with your real bank balance; it logs an adjustment transaction rather than rewriting history.

Overriding a day's balance directly

If you just want to force what a specific day's total balance shows (bypassing all the math), tap that day and use Override. Useful for correcting a known number without digging into individual transactions.

Budgets

Set a monthly limit per category in the Budgets tab — it tracks what you've actually spent against that limit for the current period.

Backup & restore

Settings → Backup Data downloads everything as a JSON file — keep a copy somewhere safe (email it to yourself, save to Drive). Restore Data from JSON loads a backup back in, replacing what's currently there.
